# 🌹 Royal Wedding Invitation: Sai & Saraswathi

A modernized, luxury, and interactive wedding invitation website built with **React**, **TypeScript**, and **Bootstrap 5**, styled with an exclusive **Royal Rose Gold & Blush** theme.

---

## 💍 Wedding Highlights

- **Couple**: **Sai & Saraswathi** (Monogram: `S & S`)
- **Auspicious Date**: **Sunday, 13th December 2026**
- **Sumuhurtham**: **Night 8:15 PM Onwards**
- **Venue & Location**: **Sri Venkateswara Royal Convention, Main Road, Kadiam, Andhra Pradesh 533126, India**

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. 💌 3D Grand Opening Wedding Card Reveal
- On first landing, visitors are greeted with a royal Indian Rose-Gold Wedding Card & Envelope.
- Features sacred invocation (*॥ శ్రీ విఘ్నేశ్వరాయ నమః ॥*), couple name, date, silk ribbon, and an embossed 3D wax seal stamped with `S & S`.
- Tapping the wax seal triggers an opening animation: the seal breaks, the top flap unfolds in 3D perspective, the golden invitation letter slides forward, and celebratory rose-gold petal confetti bursts across the screen with a victory chime.
- Includes a "Replay Card" button on the site so anyone can enjoy the opening animation again.

### 2. 🪙 Interactive Rose-Gold Scratch Card
- Canvas with shimmering Rose Gold foil and metallic stardust.
- Touch & cursor dragging to scratch off the foil.
- Real-time scratch percentage calculation.
- Auto-clears at 40% with confetti explosion and chime sound, unveiling:
  - **13th December 2026**
  - **Sunday • Muhurtham: Night 8:15 PM Onwards**
  - **Kadiam, Andhra Pradesh, India**
- Includes a "Quick Reveal" accessibility button and a "Scratch Again" reset button.

### 3. ⏳ Animated Countdown Timer
- Real-time precision countdown to **December 13, 2026 at 8:15 PM**.
- Days, Hours, Minutes, and pulsing Seconds flip-boxes styled with rose-gold glassmorphism.

### 4. 🌺 Couple Portrait Spotlight
- Ornate rose-gold framed portraits of the groom **Sai** and the bride **Saraswathi** with heartfelt blessing vows.

### 5. 📸 Cherished Moments Photo Gallery
- Responsive gallery with category filter (*All*, *Pre-Wedding*, *Candid*, *Moments*).
- Fullscreen Lightbox modal with Next/Previous touch and keyboard controls.

### 6. 📍 Venue & Location Showcase (Kadiam, Andhra Pradesh)
- Spotlight on *Sri Venkateswara Royal Convention & Garden Lawns, Kadiam*.
- "Copy Address" with immediate toast confirmation.
- "Open in Maps" direct navigation link.
- Interactive embedded Google Map centered at Kadiam, Andhra Pradesh.
- Travel Guide: Rajahmundry Airport (RJA ~ 30 mins), Rajahmundry / Kadiam Railway Stations, and NH-16 highway directions.

### 7. 🌸 Floating Rose Petals & Gold Dust
- Fullscreen ambient canvas with fluttering rose petals and subtle golden star particles.

### 8. 📱 Mobile-First Responsive Design
- Touch-action isolation prevents screen scroll while scratching on phones.
- Fluid typography using CSS `clamp(...)` for seamless readability across all phone, tablet, and desktop screens.
